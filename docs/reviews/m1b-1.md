## Round 2

S2-2 is an owner decision.

| ID | Outcome | Where |
|---|---|---|
| P2-1 | Fixed: the schema adds two rules, met by both reference boards: word-premium squares (DW, TW) in one row or column are at least 4 squares apart (`MIN_WORD_PREMIUM_DISTANCE`), and the word premiums a word can cover multiply to at most ×4 up to 6 letters and ×6 at 7 letters (`WORD_MULTIPLIER_CAPS`, via `maxWordMultiplier`). The multiplier metric was chosen over an `X.X` pattern cap because it states the intent (what a short word can score) directly and doesn't penalise harmless letter-premium spacing; the new layouts still keep `X.X` patterns at or below 12 (classic 8, casual 10; the reference boards 8).
